
### Randomized routing (randomized by the router (ext); spawns it sent to qwen)

Spawns whose router base was qwen, in the profiles the router randomized (implement); 95% bootstrap CIs from 5 judged tasks on. The router draws a share of them to another tier (`assigned: "random"`); the rest, router-assigned and run as planned (on openrouter/qwen, the model most of them ran on), is its **randomized control**. **Causal only for the intention-to-treat comparison**: the ITT row (every task the router randomized, by assignment, whatever ran) against the control. The rows by the model a randomized task ran on split it after the fact; the pre-randomization and fallback rows were chosen, not randomized, so they can differ in task difficulty.

| group | tasks | accepted/judged | acceptance (95% CI) | $/accepted (95% CI) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| randomized control (router-assigned, ran as planned) | 5 | 4/5 | 80% (40% – 100%) | 0.29 (0.15 – 0.70) |
| randomized by the router (ext), ITT: every randomized task | 8 | 5/8 | 63% (25% – 88%) | 0.56 (0.24 – 1.07) |
| randomized by the router (ext), ran on openrouter/qwen | 6 | 3/6 | 50% (17% – 83%) | 0.33 (0.15 – 1.20) |
| randomized by the router (ext), ran on anthropic/claude-sonnet-5-5 | 2 | 2/2 | 100% | 0.90 |
| not randomized: pre-randomization (no `assigned`, before the router randomized) | 3 | 2/3 | 67% | 0.53 |
| not randomized: fallback (router-assigned, ran on another model) | 1 | 0/1 | 0% | – |

1 randomized spawn(s) with another router base are not compared.
