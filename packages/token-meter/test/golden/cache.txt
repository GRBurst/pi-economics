
### Cache (Claude models)

Per session branch (warming requests refresh the cache but are no row): a request's cache write is **new context** (growth: the context added since the previous request, written once whatever happens) plus a **rewrite**, the **miss**: tokens of the prefix the previous request left in the cache (cacheRead + cacheWrite) that this one did not read. Only a miss is avoidable. Cause of a miss, the first that holds: ttl (the gap outlived the TTL in force; a gap runs from the latest send before the request to its send, ts − durationMs: a long response does not age the prefix it read; TTL: 5 min, 1 h for a main session in a mainCacheTtl 1h arm or after a 1 h write), fold (the prompt fell below 80% of the previous request's), model (the model changed), prefix (the system prompt, a prompt section or the tool list changed: the line's systemHash differs from the last one before it in the branch), other (a change of the prefix inside the TTL that the meter lines do not show: a small fold or mask, an injected agent message, a system change on lines without a systemHash; it is a real cost, not a bookkeeping residue, but its reason is not in the log). $ = API-eq at each line's input price p: a write is 1.25 p (2.0 p for a 1 h write), a read the model's read ratio × p (0.1 by default, 0.05 for Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5); a miss costs the premium of writing over reading. est. points = the calibrated quota weights (Budget) applied to the two prices a premium is the difference of: β(cacheWrite) × the $ of writing the missed tokens − β(cacheRead) × the $ of reading them; a dash without a calibration (it needs 8 complete 5-hour windows).

Folds and cold resumes (a cold resume = a request after a gap (as for ttl) beyond the TTL in force; a fold as in the cause table, unless the gap came first). Window = the event's request and the next two turn requests (cut short by the next event): premium = their misses × (write − read price). Baseline = what an ordinary request costs the same way (the mean premium of the requests outside every window, in the sessions that have such an event) once per event; net = premium − baseline.
