
### Spot checks

An accepted task re-judged by a review child. Agreement = the reviewer's own verdict (`reviewerVerdict`) and the original verdict at the draw both accept or both do not; κ = Cohen's κ over the same two classes, not estimable when one of the two never varies: spot checks are drawn from accepted tasks only, so every drawn review has an accepted original, and what remains is how often the reviewer does not accept an accepted original. The parent's verdict of the review child is no part of it. Reviewed (judged) counts every review child the parent judged; drawn = those that review a task with a `spotcheck` event, the others were not drawn.
