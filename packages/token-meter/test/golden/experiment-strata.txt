### Design v4 (3 factors, baseline `---`, 12 units with ≥ 0.05 active h)

Per stratum (the billing class of the provider of a root session's main; never pooled across strata). Effect = ratio(high level) ÷ ratio(low level) − 1 with ratio = Σ objective ÷ Σ active hours of the stratum's units at the level (ratio of sums); 95% CI from 2000 bootstrap resamples of the units of each level (seed 1); MDE = 2.80 × the bootstrap sd of the effect. Effects are marginal per factor (unadjusted: no interaction model, the other factors vary freely across the arms). A negative effect of a cost objective is cheaper.
