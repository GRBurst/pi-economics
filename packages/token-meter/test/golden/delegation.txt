### Randomized routing (randomized by the router (ext); spawns it sent to qwen)

The router draws a share of them to another tier (`assigned: "random"`); the rest, every task it assigned itself (`assigned: "router"`), whatever model ran it, is its **control**. **Causal only for the intention-to-treat comparison**: the ITT row (every task the router randomized, by assignment, whatever ran) against the pooled router-assigned row. The rows by the model that ran a task split their group after the fact (which model runs a task depends on the pool being busy or down, not on a draw): descriptive, no comparison.
